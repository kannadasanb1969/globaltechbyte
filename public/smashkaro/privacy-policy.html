1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the SmashKaro mobile application, its related websites, and our communications with you through SMS, email, push notifications and WhatsApp (together, the “Services”).

The Services are operated by Global Tech Byte Private Limited, India (“Global Tech Byte”, “we”, “us”, “our”). For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Information Technology Act, 2000, we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here.

By creating an account, using the app, or opting in to our messages, you confirm that you have read this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

2. Summary

  • We collect only the data we need to run your account, show you offers and deliver the features you ask for.
  • We message you on WhatsApp only if you have opted in, and you can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can access, correct or delete your data — see Your rights and Delete your data.
  • Questions? Write to info@globaltechbyte.com.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address, and any profile information you add.
  • Verification data: one-time passwords (OTPs) and related verification status.
  • Rewards & payout details: where a feature lets you withdraw cashback or rewards, the payout details you provide (for example a UPI ID or bank account details), used only to process your payout.
  • Support and feedback: messages, screenshots or files you send when you contact us or report an issue.
  • Referral data: referral codes you use or share.
  • Communication preferences and consent: your opt-in or opt-out choices, including the time, source and wording of your WhatsApp opt-in.

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app data: device model, operating system and version, app version, language, device identifiers (such as advertising ID or app instance ID), and crash and performance logs.
  • Usage data: screens viewed, offers or stores you open, clicks, searches, and the date and time of activity.
  • Network data: IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Transaction and tracking data: where you shop through links in the app, the referral and order-status information that partner stores return to us so we can credit rewards.

3.3 WhatsApp communication data

When you message us or receive our messages on WhatsApp, we process your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, the content of messages and any media you send, message status (sent, delivered, read, failed), timestamps, and your opt-in and opt-out records.

3.4 Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, and you should not send us over WhatsApp or any other channel: full debit or credit card numbers, card CVV/PIN, net-banking passwords, Aadhaar or other government ID numbers, or other sensitive credentials. If you send such information by mistake, contact us so we can delete it.

5. WhatsApp Business messaging

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (including the WhatsApp Business API / Cloud API), provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates (“Meta”), to communicate with you on WhatsApp. This section explains how we do that and the commitments we make under Meta's terms and policies.

5.1 Opt-in consent

  • We send you business-initiated WhatsApp messages only after you have clearly opted in to receive them.
  • Opt-in is collected through a clear, unticked consent action — for example a checkbox or toggle in the app or on a sign-up form, or when you start a conversation with us on WhatsApp — that tells you the kind of messages you will get and that they will come from SmashKaro / Global Tech Byte on WhatsApp.
  • Your consent to receive WhatsApp messages is separate and optional. You can use the app without receiving marketing messages.
  • We keep a record of when, where and how you opted in.

5.2 The types of messages we send

  • Authentication: OTPs and login or verification codes.
  • Utility: account, reward, payout and order updates that you triggered or requested.
  • Marketing: offers, deals and promotions — sent only to users who have opted in to marketing.
  • Service / customer support: replies to messages you send us. When you message us, a 24-hour customer-service window opens during which we may reply freely; outside that window we use Meta-approved message templates.

5.3 Opt-out

  • Reply STOP (or “UNSUBSCRIBE”) to any WhatsApp message from us, use the notification settings in the app, or email info@globaltechbyte.com.
  • We act on opt-out requests promptly and stop sending the relevant messages. We may still send essential transactional messages (such as an OTP you request) that are necessary to deliver a service you are actively using.
  • You can also block or report our business number directly inside WhatsApp at any time.

5.4 Our commitments under Meta's policies

We comply with the following, as updated by Meta from time to time:

  • WhatsApp Business Terms of Service
  • WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy
  • WhatsApp Commerce Policy
  • WhatsApp Business Data Processing Terms
  • Meta Platform Terms and Meta Developer Policies

In particular, we commit that we will:

  • message only people who have given valid opt-in, and honour opt-outs;
  • not send spam, unsolicited bulk messages, misleading, deceptive or harassing content;
  • not request full payment card numbers, financial account credentials, government identification numbers or other sensitive identifiers through WhatsApp;
  • not use the WhatsApp Business Platform to promote or sell prohibited, illegal or restricted goods and services;
  • use only Meta-approved message templates for business-initiated messages;
  • keep our messaging quality high — relevant, timely and expected by you — and respond to blocks and reports as required by Meta;
  • not sell, license or share WhatsApp user data with data brokers or use it for any purpose Meta does not permit.

5.5 How Meta processes your data

  • Messages sent through the WhatsApp Business Platform are received, stored and processed by Meta on our behalf so they can be delivered. This differs from a private chat between two people: because a business is the other party, the business and its service providers can read the messages you send to it.
  • Meta acts as our service provider (data processor) for this purpose under the WhatsApp Business Data Processing Terms. Meta may also process some information as an independent controller, as described in the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Your use of WhatsApp itself is governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf under contract — for example cloud hosting, analytics and crash reporting, push-notification, SMS/OTP and email providers, payment or payout partners, and Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) for message delivery.
  • Partner stores and affiliate networks, only to the extent needed to track your purchase and credit your rewards.
  • Legal and safety: where required by law, court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or Global Tech Byte, including preventing fraud.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy continuing to protect your data.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

We require service providers to protect your data and to use it only for the purposes we specify.

7. Cookies, SDKs & app permissions

The app and our websites may use cookies, local storage and software development kits (SDKs) from analytics, crash-reporting and notification providers to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure performance and understand how the Services are used. You can clear cookies in your browser and reset the advertising ID in your device settings.

The app may ask for device permissions such as notifications, camera or photo access (only when you choose to upload an image) and approximate location (only if a feature needs it). You can grant or revoke each permission at any time in your device settings; the app's core features will continue to work where the permission is not essential.

8. Data retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • After you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days, except where we must keep certain records longer.
  • Transaction, payout and accounting records are retained for the period required by Indian tax and other laws.
  • WhatsApp conversation data and consent records are retained only as long as needed to support you, prove consent and comply with law; opt-out records are kept so we can continue to respect your choice.
  • Security and fraud logs are kept for a limited period needed to protect the Services.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards — including encryption in transit, access controls, and monitoring — to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your OTPs and account details private; we will never ask you for your OTP, card PIN or password on a call or in a message.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

10. Your rights & choices

Subject to applicable law (including the DPDP Act, 2023), you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is processed;
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Erase your personal data, unless we must retain it by law;
  • Withdraw consent at any time (for example for WhatsApp, SMS, email or push marketing), as easily as it was given;
  • Grievance redressal — get your concerns answered by our grievance officer (see Contact);
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

To exercise these rights, email info@globaltechbyte.com from the email address or contact the mobile number linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity first and will respond within a reasonable time and within any period set by law. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted and operational.

Withdrawing consent may mean some features of the Services can no longer be provided.

11. Delete your data

You can ask us to delete your SmashKaro account and associated personal data (including data we hold from WhatsApp conversations) at any time.

How to request deletion

  • In the app: go to Profile → Settings → Delete account (where available).
  • By email: send a request to info@globaltechbyte.com with the subject “SmashKaro data deletion request”, mentioning the mobile number or email linked to your account.
  • By WhatsApp: message us “DELETE MY DATA” from the WhatsApp number you registered with.

We will verify your request and delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days. Records we are legally required to keep (such as payout and tax records) will be retained only for the period required, and then deleted. Your deletion request also stops all WhatsApp and other marketing messages.

12. Children's privacy
